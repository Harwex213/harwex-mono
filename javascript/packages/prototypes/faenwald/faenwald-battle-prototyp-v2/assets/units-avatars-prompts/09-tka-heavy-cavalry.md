# Тяжёлая кавалерия (ТКа)

**Роль:** кавалерия. Несокрушимая сила в открытом поле.
**Матчасть:** crème de la crème фенвальдского феодального общества. Кольчуга, наилучшая из
возможных кираса, наручи и поножи. Конь прикрыт кольчужным бардо, порой с пластинчатыми
элементами.
**Характеристики:** 120 ❤️ / 25 ⚔️ / 100 📯 / 3 гекса / 160 000.

## Вариант А — кинематографичный фотореализм (базовый)

```text
Cinematic dark-fantasy character portrait, photoreal-painterly hybrid, grounded historical realism. A high-born knight in his late thirties, aristocratic weathered face, thin scar on the cheek, damp hair pushed back, cold imperious stare. He wears the finest harness of the age: a heavy iron cuirass over a full riveted mail hauberk, articulated vambraces and pauldrons of modest size, mail voiders, all of it scratched, dented and darkened by campaign. Bands of oxidised gold filigree edge the breastplate and the neck, a tarnished gilt chain across the chest, a faded green surcoat of good wool over the plate. A great helm with a raised visor, mail coif beneath, a faded moss-green company band around the upper arm. In his right fist a long war lance held upright, the head out of the top of the frame, a broadsword at his hip. Beneath him a huge destrier in mail barding with riveted plate elements at the chest and brow, a heavy caparison of stained green cloth, steam pouring from its nostrils. Wealth shown through quality of steel, not through ornament. Muted desaturated palette: moss green, teal-grey, bone white, oxidised gold, dried-blood red. Overcast diffuse daylight, soft cool rim light from behind, no harsh highlights. Heavy atmospheric haze, drifting snow and ash specks in the air. Shallow depth of field, blurred dead-forest background. Fine film grain, gentle chromatic aberration, lifted blacks, low contrast, 35mm anamorphic look. Square 1:1 composition. Chest-up three-quarter view of the rider, head turned slightly off-axis; the horse's head and arched neck cut into the lower-left of the frame. Rider centred, fills ~60% of frame, clear headroom. Background dissolves into dark haze toward the edges, natural vignette so the portrait reads cleanly inside a UI frame. Aspect ratio 1:1. --ar 1:1
```

A high-born knight in his late thirties shown in near profile, chin level, cold imperious gaze going out of frame, face built from a few large panes of pale bone-white and cool grey glass, a thin crimson lead line for the scar on the cheek, a single amber pane for the eye, damp hair pushed back as dark shards of deep brown glass curving with the leading. ## Чем отличается от соседей по ветке

- Кольчужное бардо на коне — единственный юнит с бронированной лошадью.
- Кираса поверх кольчуги плюс золотая цепь на груди. Самый дорогой отряд в игре.
- Конь крупнее всех. Он должен занимать заметно больше нижнего левого угла, чем у ЛКа.
