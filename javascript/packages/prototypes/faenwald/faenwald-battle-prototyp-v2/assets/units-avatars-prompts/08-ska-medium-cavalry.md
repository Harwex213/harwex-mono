# Средняя кавалерия (СКа)

**Роль:** кавалерия. Баланс скорости и силы.
**Матчасть:** всадники в хороших кольчугах с защитой конечностей и закрытыми шлемами. Копья для
тарана и мечи для рубки. Ездят на реннере. Способны работать и как лёгкая, и как тяжёлая
кавалерия, но хуже узких специалистов.
**Характеристики:** 95 ❤️ / 15 ⚔️ / 90 📯 / 4 гекса / 90 000.

## Вариант А — кинематографичный фотореализм (базовый)

```text
Cinematic dark-fantasy character portrait, photoreal-painterly hybrid, grounded historical realism. A disciplined rider in his early thirties, strong jaw, short beard, sweat and road dust on his skin, steady measured gaze. He wears a full riveted mail hauberk with mail sleeves and a mail coif, quilted padding visible at the collar, iron vambraces on the forearms, a surcoat of faded green wool over the mail, dirty at the hem. A closed iron great helm rests against his chest, cradled in the crook of his arm, its eye slits dark; his head is bare but for the mail coif. A faded moss-green company band tied around the upper arm. In his right fist a heavy ash war lance held upright, the iron head just entering the top of the frame, a long sword in a leather scabbard at his hip. Beneath him a tall powerful courser with a thick neck, a padded caparison of stained cloth over its shoulders but no metal barding, breath steaming, mane wet with sleet. Well-equipped, versatile, not yet knightly. Muted desaturated palette: moss green, teal-grey, bone white, oxidised gold, dried-blood red. Overcast diffuse daylight, soft cool rim light from behind, no harsh highlights. Heavy atmospheric haze, drifting snow and ash specks in the air. Shallow depth of field, blurred dead-forest background. Fine film grain, gentle chromatic aberration, lifted blacks, low contrast, 35mm anamorphic look. Square 1:1 composition. Chest-up three-quarter view of the rider, head turned slightly off-axis; the horse's head and arched neck cut into the lower-left of the frame. Rider centred, fills ~60% of frame, clear headroom. Background dissolves into dark haze toward the edges, natural vignette so the portrait reads cleanly inside a UI frame. Aspect ratio 1:1. --ar 1:1
```

## Чем отличается от соседей по ветке

- Закрытый шлем есть, но снят и прижат к груди. Лицо остаётся видимым — это важно для аватарки.
- Конь в тканевой попоне без металла. Кольчужное бардо появляется только у ТКа.
- Кольчуга полная, кирасы нет.
