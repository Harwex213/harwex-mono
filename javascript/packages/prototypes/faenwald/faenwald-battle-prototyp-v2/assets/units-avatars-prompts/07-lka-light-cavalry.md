# Лёгкая кавалерия (ЛКа)

**Роль:** кавалерия. Скорость и добивание.
**Матчасть:** всадники в стёганых или кожаных доспехах с лёгким шлемом. Копьё и сабля. Верхом
на хобеларе, брони на лошади нет. Уязвимы для стрел.
**Характеристики:** 70 ❤️ / 10 ⚔️ / 80 📯 / 5 гексов / 40 000.

```text
Cinematic dark-fantasy character portrait, photoreal-painterly hybrid, grounded historical realism. A wiry young light horseman in his twenties, sharp windburnt face, thin stubble, hair stuck to his temple with sweat, alert restless eyes. He wears a quilted gambeson under a worn hardened-leather cuirass, straps and buckles cracked with age, a short leather-and-cloth cape at the shoulder. An open light iron helmet with a nasal bar and no visor, dented, a coarse cloth wrap under it. A faded moss-green company band tied around the upper arm. In his right fist a slender ash lance held upright, the plain head just entering the top of the frame, a curved sabre in a scuffed scabbard at his hip. Beneath him a small tough hill horse with a shaggy winter coat, no barding at all, only a plain leather bridle and a worn saddle blanket, breath steaming from its nostrils. Fast, cheap, unarmoured. Muted desaturated palette: moss green, teal-grey, bone white, oxidised gold, dried-blood red. Overcast diffuse daylight, soft cool rim light from behind, no harsh highlights. Heavy atmospheric haze, drifting snow and ash specks in the air. Shallow depth of field, blurred dead-forest background. Fine film grain, gentle chromatic aberration, lifted blacks, low contrast, 35mm anamorphic look. Square 1:1 composition. Chest-up three-quarter view of the rider, head turned slightly off-axis; the horse's head and arched neck cut into the lower-left of the frame. Rider centred, fills ~60% of frame, clear headroom. Background dissolves into dark haze toward the edges, natural vignette so the portrait reads cleanly inside a UI frame. Aspect ratio 1:1. --ar 1:1
```

## Чем отличается от соседей по ветке

- Конь голый: ни попоны с бронёй, ни кольчужного бардо. Только повод и потник.
- Шлем открытый, с наносником. Лица кавалеристов закрываются по мере утяжеления ветки.
- Конь мелкий и лохматый. У СКа и ТКа он крупнее и породистее.
