# Game Show Studio

The scene opens in a Unity-like editor (Hierarchy, Scene/Game tabs, Inspector). `?solo` shows only the Game view over the whole window, and `?t=12` freezes the scene at 12 s.

- Code builds the scene. Edits made in the editor are saved as overrides in `src/scene/scene-overrides.json` (Save button or Ctrl+S). The save endpoint exists only in `yarn dev`.
- An override is keyed by the path of names from the root, for example `Props/Host Desk`. Renaming an object or changing its parent drops its saved override. The console warns about the missing path.
- Wrap a new object in `named(object, "Name")` from `src/scene/geometry.ts`, so it shows in the Hierarchy and can be selected. Pass `folder = true` for a pure grouping node. A click in the Scene view skips folders.
- Set `userData.animated = true` on an object whose transform the animation writes every frame (the camera, the wheel rotor, the sweeping blue targets). Its transform is then left out of saves.
- Materials from `createMaterials` are shared and saved by name. A cloned material needs its own `name`.
- Undo works on snapshots of the saved diff (`SceneDocument.checkpoint`). A new kind of edit must call `checkpoint()` right before it changes the scene, or undo skips it.
- Editor Lighting (`src/engine/editorLighting.ts`) changes the scene only for the duration of one render and restores it afterwards. It dims the game lights to 0, and the light zones leave all of them out of the render. A new fake-light mesh needs `userData.beam = true` so that this mode hides it.
- Light zones (`src/engine/lightZones.ts`): each frame the lights of a room that the camera does not see are left out of the render through their layers. A light under the `Annex` group (`userData.lightZone = "annex"`) belongs to the annex. Every other light belongs to the amphitheatre. The frame time grows faster than the light count: all 32 lights cost 43 ms per frame in the wheel shot, the 21 amphitheatre lights cost 12 ms. A new light in one room does not slow down the other room, but it still costs in every shot of its own room. Keep the light count of each room low. The annex has 3 real lights; fake the rest with emissive materials.
- Blocking rule: no part may cut into another part, no two faces may z-fight, and every part must touch something that leads down to the floor. Check it with `__studio.audit()` in the browser console (`src/engine/blockingAudit.ts`): all three lists must be empty after a change to the set.
- Mark a mesh `userData.seated = true` only when it is mounted into a curved surface on purpose (a bulb in the wheel ring, a globe on a lamp neck). Mark it `userData.auditIgnore = true` only when it is not part of the set (the mirror, the city backdrop, floor decals).
- The colonnade is a polygon of straight bays (`src/scene/studio.ts`). A part that runs along the wall must use `colonnadeStrip`, so that it ends on the same radial planes as the wall slabs. A round part along a straight wall always leaves a gap or an overlap.
- Pivot rule: every selectable object has its origin at the bottom centre of its bounds (an open arc gets the nearest point of its geometry instead). `recenterPivots` (`src/engine/pivots.ts`) enforces it once at startup, so build props in any frame; `__studio.pivots()` must return an empty list.
- Camera shots: the `shot` signal in `src/state.ts` picks `wheel` (shot I) or `gameshow` (shot II). The toolbar has a button for each shot, and `?shot=gameshow` starts on that shot. The poses live in `src/scene/cameraRig.ts`; the Game Show pose is `GAMESHOW_SHOT` in `src/scene/annex.ts`. A new shot makes the camera travel for 2.4–3.2 s. The move runs in a straight line on the plan and cranes up to 7 m on the way, over every prop. `?t=` skips the travel, so a frozen frame depends only on the shot and the time.
- Annex (`src/scene/annex.ts`): a blockout room at the front right of the amphitheatre, x from -2 to 23.2, z from 9.67 to 31.4. The amphitheatre opens into it between the left wall and the right end of the colonnade. A partition at x = 23 closes the Game Show room. The casino lies behind the partition. The ceiling has the height, thickness and material of the amphitheatre ceiling: keep them equal. The annex floor is a part of `Studio/Floor/Marble`: `annexFloorPlan` cuts the exact polygon of the round floor out of it.
- Casino backdrop (`src/scene/casinoBackdrop.ts`): the Game Show camera looks along +x at Bonus Show and sees the casino through the partition opening (16 x 9 m). The engine passes the backdrop to `createAnnex`, and `mountBackdrop` places it under `Annex/Casino Backdrop`. The projector of the picture stands on the line of sight of the camera, 0.6 m behind the rest position of the camera (`PROJECTOR_SETBACK`). The camera pushes in and drifts from there, so it always stays in front of the projector. Then the photo edges stay outside the opening. The casino floor lies 0.75 m above the Game Show floor, and five steps lead up to it. The backdrop scale is the eye height of the camera above the casino floor over the eye height of the picture: (3.0 - 0.75) / 1.8 = 1.25. The furniture of the picture is then 1.25 times its drawn size. The camera height, the casino floor and the projector belong together: a change to one of them changes the scale. The backdrop room starts on the casino face of the partition (`setNear`). The backdrop room is much larger than the annex, so the casino part has no walls, ceiling or floor of its own. The engine calls `update(time)` with the scene time, so `?t=` freezes the backdrop too.
- Bonus games (`src/scene/gameProps.ts`, under `Game Props`): Bonus Show is the jester wheel, Bonus Dice is the acrylic tower, Bonus Luck is the slot cabinet. The user places them in the editor, so their places live in `scene-overrides.json`.
