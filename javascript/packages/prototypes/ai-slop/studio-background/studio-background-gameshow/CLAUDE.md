# Game Show Studio

The scene opens in a Unity-like editor (Hierarchy, Scene/Game tabs, Inspector). `?solo` shows only the Game view over the whole window, and `?t=12` freezes the scene at 12 s.

- Code builds the scene. Edits made in the editor are saved as overrides in `src/scene/scene-overrides.json` (Save button or Ctrl+S). The save endpoint exists only in `yarn dev`.
- An override is keyed by the path of names from the root, for example `Props/Host Desk`. Renaming an object or changing its parent drops its saved override. The console warns about the missing path.
- Wrap a new object in `named(object, "Name")` from `src/scene/geometry.ts`, so it shows in the Hierarchy and can be selected. Pass `folder = true` for a pure grouping node. A click in the Scene view skips folders.
- Set `userData.animated = true` on an object whose transform the animation writes every frame (the camera, the wheel rotor, the sweeping blue targets). Its transform is then left out of saves.
- Materials from `createMaterials` are shared and saved by name. A cloned material needs its own `name`.
- Undo works on snapshots of the saved diff (`SceneDocument.checkpoint`). A new kind of edit must call `checkpoint()` right before it changes the scene, or undo skips it.
- Editor Lighting (`src/engine/editorLighting.ts`) changes the scene only for the duration of one render and restores it afterwards. It dims the game lights to 0, and the light zones leave all of them out of the render. A new fake-light mesh needs `userData.beam = true` so that this mode hides it.
- Light zones (`src/engine/lightZones.ts`): each frame the lights of a room that the camera does not see are left out of the render through their layers. A light inside the casino hall box belongs to the hall, every other light belongs to the amphitheatre. The frame time grows faster than the light count: all 32 lights cost 43 ms per frame in the wheel shot, the 21 amphitheatre lights cost 12 ms. A new light in one room does not slow down the other room, but it still costs in every shot of its own room. Keep the light count of each room low.
- Blocking rule: no part may cut into another part, no two faces may z-fight, and every part must touch something that leads down to the floor. Check it with `__studio.audit()` in the browser console (`src/engine/blockingAudit.ts`): all three lists must be empty after a change to the set.
- Mark a mesh `userData.seated = true` only when it is mounted into a curved surface on purpose (a bulb in the wheel ring, a globe on a lamp neck). Mark it `userData.auditIgnore = true` only when it is not part of the set (the mirror, the city backdrop, floor decals).
- The colonnade is a polygon of straight bays (`src/scene/studio.ts`). A part that runs along the wall must use `colonnadeStrip`, so that it ends on the same radial planes as the wall slabs. A round part along a straight wall always leaves a gap or an overlap.
- Pivot rule: every selectable object has its origin at the bottom centre of its bounds (an open arc gets the nearest point of its geometry instead). `recenterPivots` (`src/engine/pivots.ts`) enforces it once at startup, so build props in any frame; `__studio.pivots()` must return an empty list.
- Camera shots: the `shot` signal in `src/state.ts` picks `wheel`, `slot`, `dice` or `gameshow`. The toolbar has a button for each shot, and `?shot=dice` starts on that shot. The poses live in `src/scene/cameraRig.ts`. A station shot is written in the station frame (`stationPoint` in `src/scene/stations.ts`, local +z points from the station to its camera). A new shot makes the camera travel for 2.4–3.2 s. On the way the camera cranes up to 7 m: this clears every prop and stays under the chandeliers (lowest point 7.5 m). `?t=` skips the travel, so a frozen frame depends only on the shot and the time.
- Casino hall (`src/scene/casino.ts`): a room in front of the amphitheatre, x from -36 to 36, z from 9.8 to 37.5, ceiling at 10 m. Every coffer beam ends on a pilaster capital, on the lintel over the opening or on the bare wall above the portal. A new beam or pilaster must keep this. The carpet stops on the exact polygon of the round marble floor.
- Photo backdrop (`src/scene/casinoBackdrop.ts`): the Game Show camera sees it through the portal in the middle of the front wall (`Casino/Walls/Front Wall/Portal`). The backdrop hangs under `Casino/Backdrop` (origin at the bottom centre of the opening, local +z into the hall), outside the hall: its near edge sits just behind the outer face of the wall. The backdrop is scaled so that its projector stands at `BACKDROP_EYE` (`src/scene/casino.ts`), the height of the Game Show camera. The projector then lies on the camera's line of sight, in front of the camera. From there the camera never sees past the photo edges. Keep three things together: the camera height, `BACKDROP_EYE`, and an opening that fits inside the photo window (about 9.7 x 5.6 m at the inner face of the wall). The engine calls the backdrop `update(time)` with the scene time, so `?t=` freezes it too.
- Stations (`src/scene/stations.ts`): Slot in the left wing, Dice in the right wing, Game Show in front of the backdrop. The Bonus Deluxe cabinet lives in `Stations/Slot`, not in `Game Props`.
